ChainDraw — Validation Sprint 3 (Web3 Gleam.io reframe)

Multi-platform verifiable engagement campaigns · 2026-08-15 · prior verdicts: pivot (0.6) → go (0.75)

Verdict: GO, with a positioning condition — confidence 0.7

The web3 quest-platform space (Galxe, Zealy, Layer3, QuestN — 23+ companies) is crowded and does not leave room for another generic "do tasks, earn crypto" product. But ChainDraw's reframe survives on two seams the incumbents don't cover: (1) trustless prize settlement — every quest platform and Gleam itself picks winners and distributes rewards as a centralized black box — and (2) platform coverage — quest platforms live on X/Discord/Telegram + on-chain tasks; nobody verifies engagement on the fediverse, Bluesky, YouTube, or Weibo. Condition: never market as a "quest platform" — the category is owned. Market as provably fair giveaways, on platforms nobody else can verify.

The open seam: "provably fair" × "multi-platform social verification" × "free-entry giveaway" is occupied by nobody. Gleam has the mechanics without the trust; quest platforms have the audience without the fairness or platform breadth; raffle dApps have the fairness without the social layer.

Demand signals

  • Organizer budget is proven at category scale: the quest-platform market sustains 23+ companies with paid campaign tooling (The Grid directory) — marketers demonstrably pay for verified social-task campaigns.
  • Web2-side demand exists too: Gleam maintains dedicated crypto-giveaway templates — the incumbent's own product line confirms campaigners want this.
  • The industry's loudest pain is verifiability: in unprotected campaigns 80%+ of participants are sybil, and "web2 social verification undermined by bots" is cited as a core weakness across the space — ChainDraw's per-platform account-verified, one-entry-per-identity model attacks the pain directly.
  • Prior sprints stand: Farcaster giveaway tools with real usage (Cast Picker, Superchain Raffle); scam-scale evidence of the trust problem; no Solana product doing free social-engagement giveaways.
